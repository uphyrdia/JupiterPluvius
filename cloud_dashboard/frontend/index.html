Connect your ESP32Integration guide ↗

Start the backend with the LAN option, then point your ESP32 at your laptop’s reachable network address. Upload readings and retrieve profiles every five seconds.

POST /api/v1/telemetryGET /api/v1/devices/{id}/recommendationPOST /api/v1/devices/{id}/ack

Use your own device ID. An ESP32 test reports profile receipt; “applied” requires an acknowledgement from MCXC444. The simulator has a reserved ID and cannot deliver profiles to hardware.
